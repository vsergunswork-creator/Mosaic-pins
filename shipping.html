Shipping & tracking

All shipments include a tracking number. We use tracked DHL parcel services whenever available for the selected destination.

  • Tracking updates may appear with a delay.
  • Your tracking number is emailed after dispatch.

Live DHL shipping rates

Shipping is calculated automatically in the cart using the current tracked DHL rate available for your destination.

  • Select your shipping country in the cart.
  • The current tracked shipping price appears before payment.
  • The destination and rate are verified again on the server during checkout.

Other destinations

For destinations outside the USA, delivery time varies by destination, customs processing and the local delivery network. The available tracked service and current shipping price are shown before payment.

Delivery estimates are not guarantees.

International orders may be subject to customs fees, VAT or import taxes. These charges are the buyer’s responsibility where applicable.
