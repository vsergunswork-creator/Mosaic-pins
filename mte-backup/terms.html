Terms of Service

Effective date: 18 September 2026

1. Purpose

MTE Backup is a private administrative tool used by the operator of Mosaic Trading Engine to create, verify, and store system backups. It is not offered as a public software-as-a-service product.

2. Authorized use

The application is intended only for the operator's own backup and recovery workflow. Google Drive authorization is used only for files managed for that purpose.

3. No trading authorization

MTE Backup does not place exchange orders, enable LIVE trading, provision production exchange credentials, or authorize the use of real money. Backup operation is separate from trading execution.

4. Availability and changes

The operator may change, suspend, or discontinue this private utility at any time. Reasonable safeguards are used to verify backup integrity, but uninterrupted availability is not guaranteed.

5. Google services

Use of Google Drive is also subject to Google's applicable terms and policies. The operator may revoke Google authorization or remove stored backup files when they are no longer required.

6. Privacy

Details about Google Drive access, data handling, retention, and security are described in the MTE Backup Privacy Policy.

7. Contact

Questions regarding MTE Backup can be sent to support@mosaicpins.space.
